TRIVIA

  • Mages who developed their powers outside of conventional Dalaran-approved teachings are called hedge mages.
  • The building of a mage's wand is not easy. Rare substances are required, and a vessel must be made that can store great quantities of magical energy.
  • Some sensitive mortals have the ability to naturally sniff out fel and arcane magic, being able to detect demons, wizards and possessed people on their own.
  • Every mage wielded the forces of magic differently, and if one was sensitive enough, one could tell the differences from one mage to the next.
  • Mages have a tendency to premature hair graying from the stress of the magical energies they wield. Despite this they can have lasting longevity, with human mages being able to survive for two hundred years, though deathly thin and frail. Aegwynn bearing a child at seven hundred fifty years old was considered an anomaly.
  • According to Muradin Bronzebeard, mages have always struck him as people of extremes, destructive but physically delicate, the metaphorical glass cannon.
    • He also mentioned that he never met a mage who didn't have twice the curiosity of a cat and less than half one's self-preservative instincts.
  • Mages automatically start at friendly standing with the Kirin Tor faction, making it possible to purchase and wear the [Tabard of the Kirin Tor] at level 1. All other classes start at neutral with the Kirin Tor.
