[INCANTATIONS]

[TYPESCRIPT ACHIEVEMENT UNLOCKED] ...

Fuck yes... seriously...

Since making incantations... there was a very, very intentional decision that was made, that simply grabed the typescript switch and flicked it off.

Basically, incantations and its definitions were... for advertising purposes only. The vscode editor error system, did not work as the return definition... was any. No matter how or what you defined within the function body itself, it got re written to any.

For several reasons but the important ones are:

  • self: this unlocked the ability to do self. The reason for this is, duplcation and all things types defintions... fucking suck. For larger projects, by the time your done... you have MANY different call sites for the same typed definition, meanwhile each call site without fail experiences some sort of drift. So now you have 8 definitions for your queue system, all different, scatterd all over your app because... you can never find them.

Self... beautifully solved this problem in addition to duplicating definitions all the while giving us a very nice and tidy way to define them at the call sites. Because lets be honest, typescript and defining them within the files... looks fucking terrible.

There are several more reasons but won't go into here, but I will say that... that one decision came with a huge bag of unrealized benefits. Performance being the most important. I have typed and defined incantations in which they included well north of 100 different ui components. And not JUST defining its variants... but also building MASSIVE variants machines. Not only that, but you can self as many deep as you want and build as wide as your hearts content.

Self... at each and every single definition... tells typescript, this is any... force waves you have no need to reivew any more type definitions past the first level... and typescript fucks off, lol.

BUUTTTTT... this benefit and the many others came with a MASSIVE tax... and it was that in itself. Any... Gained a lot, but lost a lot too.

Till now...

And it helps enormously when it comes to when typescript lies about a definition, saying it is incorrect... and there have been times that... I stare at it for a very long time, know full well... it is infact correct. DOESN"T HAPPEN OFTEN... but it still happens...

Ya... well, I'm gonna give you the defintions... while I go compile any mother fucker!

haha...

Incantations, now has the best of both worlds... and I do not know of a single solution in existance that offers this. They ALWAYS offfer one, or the other.

Meaning... we get to keep all the things we fucking love about self. The self recursion, the ability to allow us to create beautifully organized defintions and much more.

AT THE SAME FUCKING TIME, at each and every single call site you define an incantions or any other platform primtive... The editor now has access to the ENTIRE DEFINED FUNCTION BODY and all the values you placed within it. Typescript error messaging, worth flawlessly... well, not flawlessly but as designed by the provider.

NOW, this makes it so that... when we know its wrong... who the fuck cares. Because the actual defintion it will receive at runtime and compilation... is any, even though within the editor it reports differently. Which... is the perfect fucking scenario, think about it... when we code, we only care about what it says now and then in THAT EDITOR AT THAT TIME... If it reports error or now it will let us run the dev server with errors because... it thinks its any when it runs...

At that point, when it coems to us devs... who the fuck cares... does it work? GREAT!... Do we get the error reporting still, while also keeping all the benefits that were previously unlocked? YES...
